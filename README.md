# news-image-01 — Friday 13 November 2026 in One Picture

[![make.fun · news-image-01](https://make.fun/badge/news-image-01.svg)](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-01?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=news-image-01)

A newspaper front page that sums up Friday 13 November 2026's world news
in a single image: masthead "FRIDAY 13 NOVEMBER 2026" over four photo
panels, each with a short headline caption.

## The four stories

1. **Ukraine talks: Europe joins peace table** — Russia agreed to European
   participation in the next round of Ukraine peace talks; US envoys Jared
   Kushner and Steve Witkoff met Vladimir Putin in Moscow and Volodymyr
   Zelenskyy near Dublin, and work began on the war's largest prisoner
   exchange.
2. **Hormuz: tankers struck** — tankers were hit by explosions in the
   Strait of Hormuz; Iran said an LNG tanker struck a naval mine, and its
   Revolutionary Guards claimed a US early-warning radar in Bahrain was
   destroyed.
3. **Nvidia chief resigns, shares plunge 16%** — Nvidia founder Jensen
   Huang resigned as chief executive and from the board; CFO Colette Kress
   became interim CEO and the shares fell about 16%.
4. **Microsoft 365 down worldwide** — a major Microsoft 365 outage took
   Outlook mail and Teams offline for businesses, schools and government
   agencies around the world.

## How the entry works

- `entry.png` — the front-page image (JPEG bytes, 1024×1024, 252,170
  bytes, within the 1 MiB and 1024×1024 limits).
- `main.py` — a program (Python 3.13, standard library only) that copies
  `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`).
- `LICENSE` — MIT.
- `.makefun/owner.json` — claim file naming the entry wallet.
